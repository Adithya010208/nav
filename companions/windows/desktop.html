Your reminders here.

Open your saved NAV dashboard in your normal browser. This companion stays in the Windows tray and checks your reminder plan every minute.

Connect this computer

  1. Open NAV in your browser → Settings → Windows companion.
  2. Create a Windows setup and download the private setup file.
  3. Click Connect Windows below and select that file.

Only reminder details are shared. The connection cannot edit your tasks or read documents or messages. After connecting, delete the downloaded setup file.
